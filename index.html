About the Hero Atlas

A cross-curricular collection connecting AP Human Geography and ELA through place, cultural context, adaptation, and literary analysis.

Each group compares two versions of a story, investigates cultural values, and argues whether Campbell’s monomyth is a useful fit.

The map is schematic. Markers and shaded regions are approximate; connecting routes represent relationships between versions, not proven travel or diffusion paths.

Students use first names only. Group submissions remain private until teacher approval. Approved names, text, videos, transcripts, and images are publicly viewable. Contact your teacher to request corrections or removal.
